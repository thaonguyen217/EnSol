# Public Datasets

Public datasets used in this study, with their source links.

| Dataset | Used for | Source | Link |
|---|---|---|---|
| BigSolDB 2.0 | Pretraining | Krasnov et al. | https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.15094979 |
| SolProp | External evaluation | Vermeire et al. | https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.5970538 (paper: https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.2c01768) |
| Leeds | External evaluation | Boobier et al. | https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.3686213 |
| AqSolDB | Transfer-learning | Sorkun et al. | https://doi.org/10.7910/DVN/OVHAW8 |
| ESOL (Delaney) | Transfer-learning | Delaney et al. | https://github.com/deepchem/deepchem/blob/master/datasets/delaney-processed.csv |

Local, processed copies of these datasets (CSV/pickle splits used for training and evaluation) live under [`data_files/`](data_files/).
